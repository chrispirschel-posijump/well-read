About the Band

Nice to meet you.

Born in the basements and garages of Greenfield, Millcreek, Bridgeville, and Hazelwood, well-read pulls its roots from late-90s indie, emo, and punk rock. For fans of Radiohead, Explosions in the Sky, Manchester Orchestra, and American Football.

Driving guitars. Soaring melodies. Catchy choruses. We hope you'll sing along.
